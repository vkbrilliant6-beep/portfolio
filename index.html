Laser Security System

Sept 2025 – Oct 2025
Embedded C/C++ Microcontroller Sensors
PROBLEM

Small spaces need a low-cost way to detect intrusion in real time, without a full alarm-system install.

TECH

Wired a laser module, light sensor, microcontroller and buzzer, then programmed the system in embedded C/C++ to watch for a broken beam.

RESULT

The alarm triggers within 0.5 seconds of the beam being interrupted; sensor thresholds and alignment were tuned across 50 test scenarios.

LEARNED

How much reliability in a physical system comes down to careful threshold-tuning and alignment, not just correct code.
